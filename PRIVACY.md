# SnapShift Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

SnapShift does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

**What it handles.** When you press a SnapShift button or shortcut, it captures an image of the current tab, together with that tab's title and address so it can name the file and label the preview.

**Where that goes.** Nowhere outside your browser. The screenshot is kept in the extension's local storage (IndexedDB) only so the preview tab can show it. SnapShift keeps your 5 most recent screenshots and deletes older ones automatically. Removing the extension deletes them all.

**Copy and download.** Copying puts the image on your clipboard; downloading saves it to your computer. Both happen only when you ask.

**No network access.** SnapShift makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads no remote code.

**Page access.** SnapShift can only see a tab when you invoke it on that tab (Chrome's `activeTab` permission). Select area shows a temporary overlay on the page to draw your selection. For full-page captures it scrolls the page, temporarily hides or unpins fixed and sticky elements, and restores everything afterwards. It does not read or change page content otherwise.
